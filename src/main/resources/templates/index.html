How Sarvam generation works

Sarvam is prompted for established names used in the selected language, conventional English spellings, and short meanings. The app checks the returned language/gender labels and prefix, removes duplicates, and calculates the pyramid number in Java. These labels and meanings are generated by AI and should be checked before choosing a name.

Requests run in batches of 25. Cut-off or malformed responses trigger smaller follow-up batches, and complete name objects are retained. Generation stops at the target, the call/time limit, an error, or ten consecutive calls without new names. The default time budget is 15 minutes. Sarvam output varies by language, gender, prefix and account quota. The final number filter reduces the candidate list further.
